2. DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED
================================================================================

DEFECT-POST-01 severity=CRITICAL  Subledger postings never reached the general ledger balances
  evidence=before fix: AP posted 300.00/300.00 in journal lines but trial balance 0/0; payroll 2881.20 vs 0/0;
           tax 3144.20 vs 0/0. Eight call sites (AP invoice, AP payment, AR COGS, AR invoice, AR receipt,
           payroll posting, payroll payment, tax remittance) inserted status="posted" journals directly,
           bypassing PostingEngine, so AccountBalance (and the trial balance) never moved.
  fix=phase2b\posting.py post_system_journal(): one validated path for subledger journals - open period,
      journal date inside the period, active postable accounts belonging to the ledger, one-sided non-negative
      lines, balanced and non-zero, then AccountBalance updated. All eight call sites use it; PostingEngine
      shares the same validation and balance update. Business document and journal commit together, so a
      rejected posting leaves neither behind.

DEFECT-POST-02 severity=CRITICAL  Closed accounting periods accepted subledger postings
  evidence=before fix: AP invoice, payroll and tax remittance posted into a closed period (HTTP 200/201).
  fix=covered by post_system_journal (period must be open; date within period). Verified for AP, AR, inventory,
      payroll, tax and manual GL journals.

DEFECT-POST-03 severity=HIGH  Manual GL post route returned HTTP 500 on ledger rule violations
  fix=main.py maps PostingError to 409 with the rule message.

DEFECT-REF-01 severity=HIGH  No units of measure on a fresh database; products could not be created
  evidence=products.base_uom -> units_of_measure(code); the migration chain seeded none and no API creates them;
           the failure surfaced as HTTP 500 (foreign-key violation).
  fix=migration ff_seed_uom_001 seeds 19 standard units idempotently; main.py maps IntegrityError to a
      generic 409 without exposing database internals.

DEFECT-AR-01 severity=MEDIUM  Shipment COGS used the first product's accounts for every line
  fix=COGS and inventory credits are aggregated per product account pair.

DEFECT-PAY-01 severity=HIGH  Payroll payments were not limited to net pay and did not settle the liability
  evidence=the same run could be paid repeatedly; payroll_liabilities.open_amount never decreased.
  fix=payment must belong to a worker in the run, cannot exceed that worker's unpaid net pay or the open
      net-pay liability; the liability is reduced and the run marked paid when settled.

DEFECT-PAY-02 severity=MEDIUM  Payroll journals were dated "today" instead of the pay date
  fix=posting and payment journals use the pay period's pay_date (validated to fall in the accounting period).

DEFECT-INV-01 severity=HIGH  Inventory adjustments never posted to the GL
  fix=InventoryAdjust accepts an optional "accounting" block (ledger, period, offset account, date); when given,
      the adjustment posts a validated journal (inventory vs offset). Valuation now reconciles to the GL.
  open_decision=Adjustments without the accounting block are still accepted (backward compatible) and do not
      reach the GL. Decide whether unposted adjustments should be forbidden.
