# Pilot and launch
Pilot 3-5 tenants for 30-60 days. Read-only providers first. Enable one write provider per tenant at a time. Daily reconciliation and weekly incident/security review. Exit requires zero cross-tenant incidents, zero unreconciled high-risk writes, no unresolved severity-one defects, successful restore drill, accurate billing, onboarding without engineering intervention, provider SLA evidence, Copilot citation acceptance, and document-confidence thresholds. General availability requires security, privacy, legal, support, capacity, cost, provider, backup, and executive launch sign-offs.
